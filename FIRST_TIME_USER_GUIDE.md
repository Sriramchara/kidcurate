# 🌟 KidCurate — First-Time Parent Quickstart Guide

Welcome to **KidCurate**! This guide walks you through setting up your account, creating your child's profile, curating safe educational content, and locking down the experience so your children learn safely without ads, distractions, or inappropriate content.

---

## 📱 Mobile App (First-Time Walkthrough)

### Step 1: Install & Open
1. Install **`KidCurate-release.apk`** on your Android smartphone or tablet.
2. Launch **KidCurate**. On your first launch, you will be greeted by the **Welcome to KidCurate** screen.

### Step 2: Open Parent Zone
1. Tap the **`[+ Create First Profile]`** button (or tap **`Parent Zone`** at the bottom).
2. Enter your initial Parent Password / PIN to unlock the settings gate.

### Step 3: Connect Your Cloud Account
1. Under **Cloud Account**:
   - Tap **`Create Account`** if you are new (enter your Name, Email, and Password).
   - Or tap **`Sign In`** with your existing account.
2. Once signed in, all your curated videos, child profiles, and watch statistics sync across both mobile and web automatically!

### Step 4: Create a Child Profile
1. Tap **`Add Child Profile`**.
2. Enter your child's **Name**, **Age**, and choose an avatar (Compass 🦁, Sparkles 🦄, Rocket 🚀, etc.).
3. Set a simple **4-digit PIN** for your child so they can easily tap their avatar and enter their PIN to unlock their personalized adventure.

### Step 5: Curate Educational Videos
1. In the Parent Zone, go to **`Search & Add Videos`**.
2. Type any educational topic (e.g. *"Solar System for kids"*, *"Phonics sounds"*, *"Dinosaurs for preschool"*).
3. Tap **`Score with AI`** — the Gemini AI automatically evaluates the video for educational value, age appropriateness, and safety.
4. Tap **`Add to Playlist`** to assign it to your child.

### Step 6: Hand the Device to Your Child! 🎉
1. Tap the Back button to return to the **Profile Select Screen**.
2. Your child taps their avatar, enters their 4-digit PIN, and enters the **Safe Player Zone**.
3. **Child Safe Experience**:
   - 🚫 **Zero Ads**: No commercial interruptions or pop-ups.
   - 🚫 **No Suggested Videos / Rabbit Holes**: Children can only watch what you personally approved.
   - 🚫 **No Comments or External Links**: 100% safe sandbox environment.
   - ⏱️ **Parental Screen Timer**: Enforces daily viewing limits automatically.

---

## 💻 Web Portal (Desktop / Browser Walkthrough)

You can also manage everything conveniently from your laptop or PC:

1. Open your browser and go to:
   👉 **`https://kidcurate.testeenspace.site`**
2. **Sign In** with your parent email and password.
3. In the **Parent Dashboard**:
   - View your children's profiles and recent watch history.
   - Search YouTube and curate educational playlists.
   - View AI learning summaries and quizzes generated for each video.
   - Adjust screen-time limits and subject filters.

---

## 🛡️ Key Safety & Parental Features

- **Parent Gate**: Any attempt to leave the child player or open settings requires the Parent Password.
- **AI Safety Scoring**: Every video is analyzed across multiple metrics: educational depth, language safety, cognitive engagement, and age suitability.
- **Cross-Device Sync**: Any video added on the web portal appears on the child's mobile app instantly.
