# KidCurate — First-Time Parent Quickstart & Controls Guide

Welcome to **KidCurate**! This guide walks you through setting up your parent account via Google Sign-In and 6-digit Gmail OTP verification, choosing your independent Parent Lock (Password, 3x3 Pattern, or 4-Digit PIN), creating child profiles, curating safe educational playlists, and using every button and control in the app.

---

## 1. First-Time Parent Setup (`Continue with Google -> Gmail OTP -> Set Parent Lock`)

1. **Launch KidCurate**:
   - After the animated KidCurate startup intro, you arrive at the **"Who's Watching?"** home screen (and the interactive 5-page **Buttons & Controls Guide** on first launch).
2. **Open the Parent Gate**:
   - Tap **`Create First Profile`** in the center card, **`Parents`** at the top-right corner, or **`Parent Controls & Settings`** at the bottom.
3. **Sign In & Verify with 6-Digit Gmail OTP**:
   - Tap **`Continue with Google`** and pick your Google account (or tap **`Verify with Gmail OTP`** to enter your Gmail address manually).
   - KidCurate sends a **6-digit one-time verification code** to your Gmail inbox (`kidcurateservices@gmail.com`).
   - Enter the 6-digit OTP code and tap **`Verify OTP`**. First-time parents also receive an app-styled **Welcome Note** email in their inbox.
4. **Choose Your Independent Parent Lock (Password, Pattern, or PIN)**:
   - Immediately after OTP verification, KidCurate prompts you to create your **Independent Parent Lock**, completely separate from your mobile phone's lockscreen:
     - **`Password`**: At least 6 characters (recommended for strong parent-only security).
     - **`Pattern`**: Connect at least 4 dots on the interactive 3x3 grid (draw twice to confirm).
     - **`PIN`**: A 4-digit numeric PIN.
   - Your phone's device lockscreen PIN/password is **never** accepted inside KidCurate, ensuring kids who know the phone passcode cannot unlock Parent Zone or Child Lock.

## 2. Complete Guide to Buttons & Options by Screen

### Screen A: Child Profile Selection (`Who's Watching?`)
- **`Parents` Shield Button (Top Right)**: Opens the Parent Lock prompt to enter Parent Zone.
- **`Child Avatar Card`**:
  - By default (**Instant Play**), tapping a child's avatar opens their daily playlist immediately (ideal for ages 3–6).
  - If **`Require Child Profile PIN`** is enabled in the Security tab, tapping the avatar opens the 4-digit Child PIN pad.
- **`Parent Controls & Settings` (Bottom)**: Secondary shortcut to the Parent Gate.

### Screen B: Child PIN Entry Pad (When Child PIN is Enabled)
- **`Show PIN / Hide PIN` Pill**: Toggles visibility of entered digits so typos are easy to spot.
- **`Backspace` Button**: Deletes the last digit one by one.
- **`Forgot PIN? (Parent Unlock)`**: Allows a parent to unlock the child's playlist directly using the Parent Password, Pattern, or PIN.

### Screen C: Today's Curated Playlist
- **`Play All` Button**: Starts today's curated videos in sequence from Video 1.
- **`Lock / Unlock` Button (Top Right)**: Toggles **Child Lock** (engages Android screen pinning and locks navigation so the child cannot leave the playlist or switch apps without the Parent Lock).
- **`Video Queue Cards`**: Tap any video card to begin watching from that video. Shows watch progress and duration badges.
- **`Switch Profile` (Back Arrow)**: Returns to `"Who's Watching?"` (requires Parent Lock if Child Lock is active).

### Screen D: Safe Video Player (Portrait & Landscape Fullscreen)
- **`Padlock / Lock Controls` Button**:
  - Locks all player controls, timeline scrubbing, and back navigation.
  - While locked in **Portrait** or **Landscape Fullscreen**, tapping the **Left Back Arrow** or the **Unlock** button strictly prompts for your Parent Password, Pattern, or PIN.
- **`CC ON / CC OFF` Button**: Toggles **Early Reader Karaoke Phonics** subtitles with large text and synchronized golden word highlights.
- **`Fullscreen` Square Icon**: Rotates between portrait player+queue view and edge-to-edge landscape fullscreen.
- **`Next` Button**: Advances to the next approved video in the playlist.

### Screen E: Parent Zone (5 Tabs)
1. **`Profiles` Tab**:
   - **`Add Child`**: Create a child profile with Name, Age (2–14), character Avatar (`Rocket`, `Sparkles`, `Compass`, `Heart`, `Waves`, `Flame`, `Shield`, `Palette`, `Star`), and 4-digit Child PIN.
   - **`Edit` / `Delete`**: Update a child's avatar, age, or PIN, or remove a profile.
2. **`Search` Tab**:
   - **Child Selector Chips**: Pick which child profile you are adding videos to.
   - **Search Bar & Suggestions**: Search safe educational YouTube videos or paste **any YouTube link / 11-character Video ID** directly.
   - **`Add to Playlist`**: Adds the video with automatic duplicate detection and embeddability checks.
3. **`Videos` Tab**:
   - **Curated Activity Packs**: 1-tap starter bundles (Phonics, Space, Math, Science, Art) to populate a child's playlist instantly.
   - **`Up` / `Down` Arrows & `Delete` Icon**: Reorder videos in today's queue or remove a video.
4. **`History` Tab**:
   - **`Watch History`**: Shows videos watched by each child, watch duration, and completion status.
   - **`Curation Log`**: Complete audit trail of every video added or removed by the parent.
5. **`Security` Tab**:
   - **`Parent Guide: How It Works` & `Replay Interactive App Tour`**: Re-opens the 5-page visual guide of buttons and controls at any time.
   - **`App Theme`**: Switch between `Light`, `Dark`, or `System` theme.
   - **`Daily Screen Time & Bedtime Curfew`**: Choose `30 min`, `45 min`, `60 min`, `90 min`, or `Unlimited` daily viewing, and configure automatic `Bedtime Curfew` hours (e.g., `8 PM` to `7 AM`).
   - **`Reading & Subtitles`**: Toggle `Early Reader Karaoke Phonics` and `Enable Subtitles by Default`.
   - **`Child Access & Video Quality`**: Toggle `Require Child Profile PIN` (vs. Instant Play) and select default streaming resolution (`Auto`, `1080p`, `720p`, `480p`, `360p`).
   - **`Parent Cloud Account`**: View connected Google account, `Sign Out`, or permanently `Delete Account & Cloud Data`.
   - **`Parent Lock & Security`**: Switch your independent Parent Lock style at any time between **`4-Digit PIN`**, **`Pattern`**, and **`Password`**, or toggle optional **`Unlock with Fingerprint`** (hardware fingerprint only; phone screen lock PIN is never accepted).
