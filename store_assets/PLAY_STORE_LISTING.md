## 1. App Title & Descriptions

### App Title (Max 30 characters)
```text
KidCurate: Safe Kids Videos
```

### Short Description (Max 80 characters)
```text
100% vulgarity-free, parent-curated stories, crafts, and videos for children.
```

### Full Description (Up to 4,000 characters)
```text
KidCurate is a wholesome entertainment sanctuary for children, built by parents for parents. 

Even on children’s video platforms, unexpected adult humor, vulgar slang, scary screamers, and algorithmic brainrot can sneak through. KidCurate eliminates that anxiety by placing parents entirely in control of their child’s viewing experience.

No comments. No toxic algorithmic rabbit holes. No disruptive advertisements. Just heartwarming moral stories, innocent creative fun, and calm entertainment hand-picked or approved by parents.

🌟 WHY PARENTS CHOOSE KIDCURATE:

🛡️ 100% PARENT-CONTROLLED CURATION
Playlists are never dictated or locked by arbitrary age algorithms. You choose what your child watches. Pick from instant, hand-curated Activity Packs or search and add specific videos you approve of.

❤️ HEARTWARMING MORAL VALUES & STORIES
Inspire your children with timeless fables and moral stories teaching kindness, honesty, courage, patience, helping others, and respecting elders (including Panchatantra, Aesop's fables, and gentle bedtime tales).

🎨 INNOCENT CREATIVE FUN & CRAFTS
Nurture your child’s imagination with follow-along cartoon drawing lessons, supersonic origami paper planes, gentle baby animal exploration, and fun physical dance-alongs.

⚡ INSTANT 1-CLICK PARENT LIFESAVER PACKS
Need to cook dinner or answer an urgent email? Launch curated 20-30 minute packs with a single tap:
• Heartwarming Moral Stories (Tales of kindness and honesty)
• Hands-On Crafts & Origami (Paper gliders & drawing)
• Dinner Prep Quiet Time (Safe, engaging stories while parents cook)
• Wiggle & Burn Energy (Gentle kids' yoga & indoor movement)
• WOW Inventions & Science (Curiosity and magic tricks)
• Calm Bedtime Wind-Down (Soothing storybooks and lullabies)

🔒 BUILT-IN PARENTAL LOCKS & CURFEW
• Master Parent PIN: Child Lock prevents little fingers from exiting the player or wandering into settings.
• Daily Screen-Time Limit: Automatically pauses playback when daily watch time is reached.
• Bedtime Curfew: Automatically sleeps during nighttime hours to encourage healthy sleep habits.

🌐 SEAMLESS PARENT WEB PORTAL & MOBILE SYNC
Manage your family from anywhere. Use the web dashboard or Android Parent Zone to organize playlists, review watch history, and add stories across multiple children in your household.

👶 DESIGNED STRICTLY FOR FAMILIES (COPPA COMPLIANT)
• Zero third-party advertisements or trackers.
• No behavioral profiling, comments, or public feeds.
• Complete privacy protection for your children.

Give your children a peaceful, joyful screen experience you can trust. Download KidCurate today!
```

| Question | Answer | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Is all user data encrypted in transit?** | **Yes** | TLS/HTTPS encrypted. |
