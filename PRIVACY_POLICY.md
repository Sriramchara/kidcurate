# KidCurate Privacy Policy & Legal Disclosures

**Last Updated:** November 16, 2026

KidCurate ("we", "our", or "the App") is an educational, parent-controlled video curation platform designed to provide children with safe, curated access to wholesome YouTube videos without algorithmic rabbit holes, comments, or external web browsing.

Protecting family and children's privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains our collection, use, and disclosure of information in compliance with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K)**, and Google API / YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

---

## 1. Compliance with YouTube API Services Terms & Google Privacy Policy

KidCurate utilizes the **YouTube Data API v3** to search for public YouTube videos and the **YouTube Embedded Player** to stream educational video content.

By using KidCurate, you acknowledge and agree that:
1. KidCurate uses **YouTube API Services**.
2. Users are bound by the **YouTube Terms of Service**, which can be reviewed at:  
   👉 [https://www.youtube.com/t/terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)
3. Google’s processing of data in connection with YouTube services is governed by the **Google Privacy Policy**, which can be reviewed at:  
   👉 [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### YouTube Data & Player Restrictions
* **No Video or Audio Downloading:** KidCurate never downloads, rips, or records `.mp4` or audio files from YouTube servers.
* **Official Embedding Only:** Video playback is conducted entirely through YouTube’s official embedded player technology.
* **No Background Play:** The player pauses automatically when the app is minimized, closed, or when the screen turns off.
* **Creator Attribution:** Every video in KidCurate displays the original YouTube creator channel name.
* **No Modification of Ads:** KidCurate does not block, obscure, or alter advertisements served by YouTube on embeddable videos.

## 2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

KidCurate is strictly a **parent-directed curation tool**. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK).

### Information Provided by Parents:
* **Parent Account Details:** Parent name, parent email address, and encrypted password hash (used for cloud sync and parental authentication).
* **Child Profile Details (Parent-Managed):** Child's first name/nickname, age, chosen character avatar badge, and an offline PIN.
* **Curated Playlists:** YouTube Video IDs, titles, durations, and parent-assigned playlist sequencing.
* **Watch History / Progress:** Video playback timestamps stored locally and synchronized to the parent's account.

### Information We NEVER Collect from Children:
* ❌ Real full names, physical addresses, or phone numbers.
* ❌ GPS or precise geolocation data.
* ❌ Biometric data, camera photos, or microphone recordings.
* ❌ Contacts or device identifiers for targeted advertising.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

The limited information collected is used solely to:
1. Allow parents to create and manage safe viewing playlists for their children.
2. Authenticate the parent during access to the Parent Zone.
3. Keep track of which videos in the playlist have been completed.
4. Synchronize playlist data across the parent's authorized devices.

We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize any user or child data. We do **not** serve third-party behavioral advertisements.

---

## 4. Parental Rights & Data Deletion

Under COPPA and GDPR (Right to Erasure), parents have complete control over their family’s data at all times:
* **Review & Modify Data:** Parents can view and update child profiles and playlists directly in the Parent Control Center.
* **Delete Child Profiles:** Parents can delete any child profile and associated playlist history under **Parent Zone → Profiles**.
* **Complete Account Erasure:** Parents can permanently delete their parent account, all child profiles, playlists, and cloud data at any time under **Parent Zone → Settings → Parent Cloud Account → Delete Account**, or by making a request to our support email.

---

## 5. Security

All communications between the KidCurate application and backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS). Passwords are encrypted using salted cryptographic hashes (bcrypt) and are never stored in plaintext.
